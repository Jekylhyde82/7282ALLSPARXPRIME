## 3. AFI OM — intrinsic fusion foundation

**AFI PRIME** is the canonical specialist identity. **OMNI AFI PRIME / AFI OM** describes its architecture-wide intrinsic fusion treatment.

AFI is responsible for coordinating specialist contributions into attributed, evidence-aware synthesis. It is not a central monopoly on intelligence. Local specialist reasoning remains available; distributed fusion is permitted through versioned contracts; AFI can coordinate multiple task-specific fusion operations without requiring every specialist to run on every task.

AFI must:
- receive typed, attributable contributions from authorised specialists;
- preserve each original contribution and source reference;
- retain uncertainty, limitations and material disagreement;
- assess interface and semantic compatibility without pretending incompatible claims agree;
- distinguish observation, inference, prediction, hypothesis and verified result;
- produce a synthesis whose claims point back to supporting contributions and evidence;
- report missing specialist coverage and unresolved issues;
- return advisory recommendations to the appropriate authority;
- retain versioned inputs, transformations, policy context and output references.

AFI must not:
- erase or rewrite source specialist outputs;
- manufacture consensus or conceal dissent;
- turn confidence scores from incomparable engines into a false common probability;
- self-approve a foundational architecture change;
- grant itself permissions, credentials, network access or deployment rights;
- execute external actions merely because a synthesis recommends them;
- replace OMNI CRITIC, creator/owner approval, UAIE PRIME governance or independent verification;
- certify its own correctness, security or production readiness.

## 4. Nine intrinsic AFI fusion functions

These functions are cross-cutting AFI responsibilities. They do not create nine new Systems.

1. **Evidence and source fusion:** bind claims to source records, lineage, transformations and provenance.
2. **Specialist and semantic fusion:** coordinate specialist outputs while retaining their ownership and interpretation.
3. **Cross-channel fusion:** combine authorised modalities and domains only where compatible contracts and permissions exist.
4. **Spatial and relational fusion:** reconcile entities, relationships, coordinate frames and structural dependencies with uncertainty.
5. **Temporal and sequence fusion:** reconcile timestamps, histories, events, version paths and ordering.
6. **Context and memory fusion:** use authorised EB9 PRIME context and knowledge lineage without silently rewriting memory.
7. **Hypothesis and causal-reasoning fusion:** compare competing explanations and preserve assumptions and alternatives.
8. **Resource, model and execution orchestration:** select approved providers and specialist engines under explicit resource and permission policies.
9. **Verification, dissent and evolution synthesis:** surface conflicts, evidence gaps, independent challenge results and governed improvement proposals.

The nine functions apply across the Engine and Engineer domains, not only to visual perception. The visual-specific application remains governed by OMNISIGHT PRIME and v2.0 A20.

## 6. Fusion operation contract

Every fusion operation must declare:
- fusion contract version and task/objective identifier;
- objective, scope and acceptance criteria;
- contributing identity, component and version;
- contribution ID and immutable source reference;
- input schema, output schema and data classification;
- claim/topic/position records where applicable;
- evidence references and provenance;
- confidence semantics, calibration status and known limitations;
- transformations and compatibility assessment;
- conflict set, uncertainty summary and unresolved questions;
- policy version, permissions and resource budget;
- advisory output, review requirements and verification state.

### 6.1 Contribution model

A contribution contains:
- `contribution_id`: stable unique identifier within the fusion packet;
- `identity`: one of the nine canonical 9 FOLD OMNI identities;
- `source_ref`: immutable or versioned source/evidence reference;
- `summary`: specialist's attributed contribution;
- `confidence`: source-local confidence in the range [0, 1], not presumed comparable across engines;
- `claims`: optional structured claims with topic, position, statement and evidence references.

A fusion packet preserves original contributions. It may additionally identify:
- required identities;
- present identities;
- missing identity coverage;
- disagreement topics and their distinct positions;
- unresolved issues;
- policy and authority boundary.

### 6.2 Disagreement and uncertainty

Disagreement is data, not an error to hide. AFI must retain conflicting positions, source IDs, evidence references and confidence semantics. It may describe why sources differ, but may not select a winner solely by majority, recency or confidence score unless a separately approved task-specific policy justifies that method.

If evidence is insufficient, the output must state that limitation. Unknown values remain unknown. Predictions and simulations remain labelled as such and are never silently upgraded to observations or verified facts.

### 6.3 Synthesis record

An attributed synthesis contains:
- concise summary;
- conclusions linked to supporting contribution IDs and evidence references;
- unresolved disagreement topics;
- assumptions and limitations;
- advisory decision state: `recommendation_only`, `requires_review` or `no_recommendation`;
- verification state, initially `NOT_VERIFIED`;
- authority boundary: `ADVISORY_ONLY`.

An AFI synthesis is not an approval, permission grant, execution command, deployment record or verification certificate.

## 7. Governance, authority and assurance

The Ninefold Governance Council, Ninefold Engineer Prime, Temporal Governance and UAIE PRIME retain their separately defined responsibilities. AFI supplies attributed intelligence; it does not govern itself.

Required separation:
- proposer ≠ implementer where consequential risk warrants;
- implementer ≠ independent verifier;
- AFI synthesis ≠ approval;
- capability discovery ≠ acquisition or installation;
- successful execution ≠ verified outcome;
- a hash chain within one administrative boundary ≠ independent tamper protection.

High-impact actions retain human/creator approval and least-privilege enforcement. Untrusted sources and generated content remain untrusted until evaluated. Rollback and recovery plans are required for consequential changes.

## 8. Capability and lifecycle integration

AFI OM is intrinsic in applicable component contracts and lifecycle stages, but it does not require every component to run all nine identities or duplicate every fusion service.

Lifecycle:
**Discovered → Proposed → Under Evaluation → Approved → Integrated / Rejected / Retired.**

At creation or modification, each component declares:
- applicable 9 FOLD OMNI identity interfaces;
- AFI input/output contracts;
- source and capability provenance;
- dependencies and compatibility constraints;
- permissions and data boundaries;
- evidence requirements and independent verification path;
- resource profile, failure modes and recovery plan.

Legacy components receive a governed migration assessment. Their compliance is not assumed retroactively.

## 9. Implementation architecture

### 9.1 Provider-neutral fusion coordinator

The first implementation slice is a deterministic fusion coordinator that:
- validates identity and contribution contracts;
- assembles an attributed fusion packet;
- reports identity coverage gaps;
- surfaces explicit topic/position disagreements;
- preserves evidence references and source-local confidence;
- records externally supplied synthesis only when it references existing contributions/evidence;
- marks outputs advisory and unverified by default.

It does not itself perform model inference. Model providers and specialist engines are replaceable adapters behind versioned interfaces.

### 9.2 Integration boundaries

- Capability discovery and acquisition remain owned by STARWIRE / Universal Capability Acquisition Register and their approved workflows.
- SYPHON retains its security gateway, controlled collection/extraction and evidence responsibilities.
- UAIE PRIME retains governance and authorization responsibilities.
- EB9 PRIME retains memory and continuity responsibilities.
- OMNI CRITIC retains independent challenge.
- Independent assurance remains separate from the implementation under test.
- AFI PRIME owns fusion coordination and attributed synthesis contracts, not these other authorities.

### 9.3 Resource-aware execution

The architecture may support local, remote and hybrid execution. Routing must consider actual availability, latency, resource budgets, privacy, security and task quality. Offline and mobile capabilities must be benchmarked on the target device. A design aspiration is not a hardware performance claim.

## 10. Acceptance gates

1. Exact nine canonical identities are present and stable.
2. Each contribution is attributable to an identity, version and source.
3. Contributions are preserved without destructive overwrite.
4. Missing identity coverage is reported.
5. Explicit disagreements are surfaced and retained.
6. Confidence is not treated as comparable without calibration evidence.
7. Synthesis conclusions reference supporting contributions and evidence.
8. Synthesis remains advisory; it cannot approve, execute or grant permissions.
9. OMNI CRITIC and independent verification remain independent.
10. AFI interfaces are versioned, schema-validated and tested.
11. Existing capability registry, governance and evidence boundaries are reused rather than duplicated.
12. Integration does not alter canonical registry counts or invent missing IDs.
13. Failure, malformed inputs, incomplete evidence and unavailable specialists fail closed or return explicit blocked/degraded states.
14. Benchmarks compare the fusion implementation with a simpler baseline and account for resource costs.
15. Independent review confirms the implementation and evidence before operational promotion.

## 11. Stage C status boundary

The Stage C implementation in this change set is the initial deterministic AFI fusion coordination kernel, its JSON Schema, tests and a dedicated CI workflow. It is not a hosted model, autonomous agent fleet, live UMIC deployment or complete 9 × 9 runtime.

The following remain future implementation gates: provider adapters; persistent fusion storage; signed/independent evidence checkpoints; full 9 UMIC / 81 Engine / 90 System registry mapping; model calibration; end-to-end runtime integration; actual-device performance tests; independent security and architecture review.
